# Game Provider Questionnaire

Obtain written answers before production dependence.

## Publisher/business
1. Will a new low/no-traffic domain be accepted?
2. What exact revenue-share formula applies to this publisher?
3. What costs/deductions may be taken before publisher share?
4. What are payout threshold, currency, schedule, supported payout countries, and hold/review rules?
5. Are there minimum traffic, quality, or geographic requirements?

## Catalog access / automation
6. Does the publisher account provide an official JSON API, RSS/XML feed, catalog export, webhook, or other bulk synchronization method?
7. If yes, what authentication, rate limits, pagination, update timestamps, and usage limits apply?
8. Is automated periodic synchronization permitted?
9. How are removed, disabled, expired, or updated games signaled?
10. If no bulk feed/API is available, what approved alternative should a gaming portal use: Direct Game Integration, white-label, account-manager export, or another method?
11. Is scraping the public catalog prohibited? UTOON's default is not to scrape without explicit permission.

## Content rights
12. May UTOON embed each supplied game on `utoon.net`?
13. May UTOON use supplied thumbnails/screenshots/art in game cards, SEO pages, social previews, and promotions?
14. May UTOON write its own titles/descriptions/how-to-play text while preserving required game/developer attribution?
15. May UTOON localize surrounding metadata/UI into Arabic and other languages?
16. What happens when a game or asset is removed/relicensed?

## Integration/measurement
17. What iframe/SDK events are officially available?
18. What analytics/revenue data is exposed by game, country, device, and date?
19. Are there restrictions on publishing independent compatibility/performance observations?
20. What health/error reporting or broken-game mechanism exists?
21. What exact referrer/query parameters are required for valid monetization attribution?

## Ads/privacy/safety
22. Which party is responsible for consent collection and age/minor-related ad treatment?
23. What cookies/storage/tracking can provider games or ads create?
24. What redirect/pop-up behavior is allowed, and how are malicious/unsafe ads escalated?
25. What CSP/iframe sandbox/fullscreen/permissions settings are supported?

## Future app
26. Is distribution inside a Google Play/native Android app permitted?
27. If special written approval is required, what technical/commercial conditions apply?

Store contracts/private replies outside the public repository; record only non-confidential decisions/status here.
