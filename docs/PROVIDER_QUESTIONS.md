# Game Provider Questionnaire

Obtain written answers before production dependence.

## Publisher/business
1. Will a new low/no-traffic domain be accepted?
2. What exact revenue-share formula applies to this publisher?
3. What costs/deductions may be taken before publisher share?
4. What are payout threshold, currency, schedule, supported payout countries, and hold/review rules?
5. Are there minimum traffic, quality, or geographic requirements?

## Content rights
6. May UTOON embed each supplied game on `utoon.net`?
7. May UTOON use supplied thumbnails/screenshots/art in game cards, SEO pages, social previews, and promotions?
8. May UTOON write its own titles/descriptions/how-to-play text while preserving required game/developer attribution?
9. May UTOON localize surrounding metadata/UI into Arabic and other languages?
10. What happens when a game or asset is removed/relicensed?

## Integration/measurement
11. What iframe/SDK events are officially available: load, start, pause, game-over, ad-start/end, errors?
12. What analytics/revenue data is exposed by game, country, device, and date?
13. Are there restrictions on publishing independent compatibility/performance observations or benchmarking?
14. What health/error reporting or broken-game mechanism exists?

## Ads/privacy/safety
15. Which party is responsible for consent collection and age/minor-related ad treatment?
16. What cookies/storage/tracking can provider games or ads create?
17. What redirect/pop-up behavior is allowed, and how are malicious/unsafe ads escalated?
18. What CSP/iframe sandbox/fullscreen/permissions settings are supported?

## Future app
19. Is distribution of these games inside a Google Play/native Android app permitted?
20. If special written approval is required, what technical/commercial conditions apply?

Store contracts/private replies outside the public repository; record only non-confidential decisions/status here.
